## Why this license, and not MIT

Earlier versions of this project used the MIT License, which allows
unrestricted commercial use and resale. That's no longer the intent — this
tool represents real, ongoing effort, and it's meant to be freely usable by
individuals and teams for their own work, not something a third party
repackages and sells. PolyForm Noncommercial is a standard, unmodified,
purpose-built license for exactly that split, rather than a custom
homemade one.
